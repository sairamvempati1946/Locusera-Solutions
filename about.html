About LOCUSERA

We are a specialist recruitment partner built on industry expertise, not keyword matching.

Our Story

LOCUSERA SOLUTIONS PRIVATE LIMITED was founded on a simple observation: generalist recruiters were failing highly specialised industries. Engineering leaders were drowning in irrelevant CVs, and pharmaceutical companies were struggling to find candidates who truly understood GMP compliance.

We built LOCUSERA to fix that. Instead of trying to be everything to everyone, we focused entirely on three verticals: IT, Pharma, and Digital Marketing. We hired recruiters who come from these industries, gave them the tools to source passive talent, and built a rigorous scorecard-driven screening process.

Today, we help some of the most innovative companies in the world build teams that drive their businesses forward- and we do it with a level of care and compliance that our clients have come to rely on.

We don't spray CVs. We deliver small, highly qualified shortlists that you can actually hire from.
