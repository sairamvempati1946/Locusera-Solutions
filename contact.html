Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about working with LOCUSERA.

How do I apply for a job?
Browse our Careers page, find a role that matches your skills, and click "Apply Now". You can submit your details directly to our WhatsApp team.
Do you charge candidates a fee?
No. LOCUSERA is a client-funded recruitment agency. We never charge candidates for finding them a role or for submitting their resume.
How quickly will you respond?
If you contact us via WhatsApp, we typically respond within a few hours during business hours. If you use the contact form or email, we reply within 24 hours.
What industries do you specialize in?
We focus exclusively on three verticals: IT (engineering, cloud, data), Pharma (QA, QC, R&D, regulatory), and Digital Marketing (growth, SEO, paid media).
I'm an employer. How do I start hiring?
Visit our For Employers page and fill out the hiring request form. Our team will contact you within 24 hours to discuss your requirements.
